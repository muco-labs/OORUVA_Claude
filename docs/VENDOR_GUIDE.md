# Vendor guide

For stall owners listing on OORUVA. App name on your phone: **OORUVA Vendor**.

## Signing up

1. Open the app and choose **I'm a vendor**.
2. Enter the phone number your customers already call. Tap **Send OTP**.
3. Type the six digits from the SMS. If it does not arrive, wait for the
   30-second timer and tap **Resend code**.

*While the app is in testing, any ten-digit number and any six digits will get
you in — no real SMS is sent yet.*

## Listing your business

You will be asked for:

- **Business name** — what is painted on the cart, not a formal company name
- **Owner name**
- **Category** — chai, food, juice, shop, service, salon
- **Location** — stand at your stall when you set this, so customers walking
  past actually find you
- **Address** — a landmark helps more than a postcode
- **Opening hours** — keep these honest; a closed stall marked open loses trust
- **Description** — one or two sentences, what you are known for

## Products

Add each item with a name, price and photo. Prices are what a customer pays
today — change them in the app when they change at the stall. Photos taken in
daylight, close, on a plain surface, do far better than dark evening shots.

## Photos

Upload at least three: the stall itself, the counter, and your best-selling
item. Tap any photo to set it as the **main** one — that is what appears in
search results, so choose the one that makes someone hungry.

## FSSAI

The app asks whether you have an FSSAI certificate.

- **Yes** — enter the 14-digit number and upload a photo or PDF. It goes to a
  private store; only the OORUVA verification team can open it.
- **No** — tap the WhatsApp button and the team will help you register. The
  assisted fee is **₹750 plus GST**, paid to the registration service, not to
  OORUVA.

Cooked food generally requires FSSAI. Packaged goods and non-food stalls
generally do not. If you are unsure, choose "No" and ask on WhatsApp.

## Verification

After you submit, your listing shows **Under verification**. A person reads it
within 48 hours and either:

- **approves** — you appear to customers straight away
- **requests changes** — you get a note saying what to fix; edit and resubmit
- **rejects** — you get a reason

You cannot be found by customers until you are approved.

## Your dashboard

Once live you see: today's check-ins, unique customers and revenue; a seven-day
footfall chart; and quick links to edit your business, manage photos, and open
analytics.

**Reviews.** Every review is visible to you, and you can reply once to each.
A calm, specific reply to a bad review reads better to future customers than
no reply at all. You cannot delete a review — flag it only if it is abusive or
untrue, and a moderator will look.

**Offers.** Create a discount with a title, amount and validity date. It appears
in the customer app and in your redemption count.

## Getting help

The WhatsApp support number is in the app under Help. Message it with your
business name and what you are stuck on.
