# Customer guide

For people using OORUVA to find street food. App name on your phone: **OORUVA**.

## Getting in

Open the app, choose **I'm a customer**, enter your phone number, and type the
six-digit code you receive.

*In testing, any ten-digit number and any six digits work — no real SMS yet.*

## Finding somewhere to eat

**Home** lists what is around you. Search by name or by what you want —
"chai", "samosa", "repairs" — or tap a category chip to narrow it down.

Each card shows the rating, opening hours, distance, and whether the stall is
open now. Tap the heart to save it; tap the card to open it.

**Map** shows the same stalls as pins, nearest first underneath.

## A stall's page

Photos, address, hours, phone, the menu with prices, and what other people said.
Two buttons matter:

- **Check in here** — tap it when you are actually at the stall. It earns you
  50 points and tells the owner someone came.
- **Heart** — saves it to your list.

## Group finder

The one worth knowing about. Tell it how many of you there are and what you have
to spend, and it works out the per-person budget and shows the stalls that fit.

Five people, ₹1500 → ₹300 each → three places that work at that price.

## Points

| What you do | Points |
|---|---|
| Check in at a stall | 50 |
| Write a review | 10 |
| Add a photo | 5 |
| Create a post | 2 |

100 points is worth about ₹20 in vouchers. **Rewards** shows your balance, how
far you are from the next tier, and what you have earned recently.

Points appear as *pending* first and become spendable once verified. Checking in
somewhere you are not, or reviewing the same stall repeatedly, gets them
rejected — the scheme is checked.

## Reviews and posts

Rate one to five stars, write a line, add a photo. Be specific: "the vada is
fresh after 5pm" helps someone; "good" does not. You can review a stall once,
and edit it later.

**Feed** shows what other people found. Like, comment, or post your own
discovery and tag the stall.

Owners can reply to your review. They cannot delete it.

## Your profile

Check-ins, reviews and saved stalls, plus settings and support.

## Trouble

Help and support is in your profile. If a listing is wrong — closed down, wrong
hours, wrong location — flag it there and someone will check.
